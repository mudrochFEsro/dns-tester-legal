DNS/HTTP Tester

Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-06-19

DNS/HTTP Tester ("the App") is a network diagnostic tool. We take your privacy seriously.

Data Collection

The App does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers. All operations run locally on your device.

Local Storage

Query history and your saved Watchlist domains with their result snapshots (each up to 50 entries) are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time. Exported reports (TXT/JSON/CSV) are written only when you ask and are handed to the system share sheet — never uploaded by the App.

Third-Party Services

The App makes requests to public DNS-over-HTTPS resolvers:

  • Cloudflare (1.1.1.1)
  • Google (8.8.8.8)
  • Quad9 (9.9.9.9)
  • Mozilla
  • Wikimedia

For SSL checks and subdomain discovery: crt.sh. For WHOIS: rdap.org and api.whois.vu. For hosting location/ASN: ipapi.co (the resolved IP address is sent — no personal identifier). Only the domain name you query (and, for the location feature, its resolved IP) is sent.

Web version only: browsers block cross-origin requests, so some lookups are routed through public CORS proxies (corsproxy.io, api.codetabs.com). The native iOS/Android apps connect directly and use no proxy.

Analytics & Tracking

The App does not use any analytics, crash reporting, advertising SDKs, or tracking.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
